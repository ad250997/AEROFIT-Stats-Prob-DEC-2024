    <h1>Aerofit Treadmill Customer Segmentation Analysis</h1>
  
  <h2>Overview</h2>
  <p>This case study analyzes customer data to identify target demographics and preferences for Aerofit's three treadmill models: KP281 (entry-level), KP481 (mid-level), and KP781 (premium). The dataset includes 180 customer records with features like age, gender, income, fitness goals, and product choice.</p>

  <h2>Key Objectives</h2>
  <ul>
      <li>Segment customers based on product preferences.</li>
      <li>Identify demographic and behavioral trends for each treadmill model.</li>
      <li>Provide actionable recommendations for marketing and sales strategies.</li>
  </ul>

  <h2>Methodology</h2>
  <ul>
      <li>Performed univariate and multivariate analysis on numerical and categorical features.</li>
      <li>Used correlation heatmaps and pairwise plots to identify relationships between variables.</li>
      <li>Conducted product-wise segmentation using conditional probabilities and statistical summaries.</li>
  </ul>

  <h2>Key Insights</h2>
  <ul>
      <li><strong>KP281 (44.4% market share):</strong> Preferred by entry-level users with average income of $46k. Targets customers aged 23–33, with 52.6% female buyers.</li>
      <li><strong>KP481 (33.3% market share):</strong> Appeals to intermediate users (avg. income $49k). Balanced gender preference and fitness ratings of 1–4.</li>
      <li><strong>KP781 (22.2% market share):</strong> Dominated by high-income males (avg. $75k) with advanced fitness goals (93.5% of rating-5 customers). Strong correlation between education and income for this segment.</li>
  </ul>

  <h2>Recommendations</h2>
  <ul>
      <li>Target KP281 marketing toward younger, budget-conscious customers.</li>
      <li>Promote KP781 through premium channels focusing on high-income, fitness-oriented males.</li>
      <li>Leverage KP481’s balanced appeal for cross-demographic campaigns.</li>
  </ul>
